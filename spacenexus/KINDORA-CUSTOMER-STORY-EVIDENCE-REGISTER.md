# Kindora customer-story evidence register

**Purpose:** Collect verifiable source material and publication permissions before writing or publishing customer stories.

**Current state (2026-11-26):** No approved customer stories, attributable customer quotes, outcome records, or publication permissions were present in the reviewed project materials. Do not publish placeholder stories or infer customer results from product demos.

## Publication gate for every story

- Customer legal name, or an explicitly approved anonymized description.
- Written permission for the story, quoted material, customer identity, and any customer-provided data used.
- Customer contact and permission record location, approver, scope, and date.
- Problem and baseline period; implementation date and scope; Kindora modules used.
- Measured result, before/after values, measurement period, data owner, calculation/methodology, and source record.
- Attributable quote, exact approved wording, named speaker and role (unless anonymization is approved), and approval date.
- Story author, editorial reviewer, datePublished, dateModified, and URLs of relevant Kindora feature pages.
- Customer-specific context and limitations stated beside the result; no universal guarantee language.
- Article/BlogPosting JSON-LD checked against the visible story.
